01 · Territorial analysis · August 2026

The retail gap

Question
Where is there money that local retail is not capturing?
Result
Out of 277 municipalities, only Rincón de la Victoria and Antequera combine a large market, high income and too little retail, confirmed by three independent measures.

02 · Fuel stations · August 2026

The discount illusion

Question
Is your loyalty-card discount real, or does it only offset a higher starting price?
Result
Of 47 assessable official plans, 3 go below the price a driver already pays without a card at a low-cost station. None of them belongs to an oil company.
  • SQL
  • Looker Studio
  • MITECO
Case study (ES)

03 · Market analysis · August 2026

Legal holiday rentals in Barcelona

Question
Where should the remaining HUTB licences be concentrated before November 2028?
Result
After correcting the metric, the total barely changes ($244.0M vs $241.5M), but the ranking of neighbourhoods does (rank correlation 0.31).
  • SQL
  • Looker Studio
  • Inside Airbnb
Case study (ES)

04 · Pricing · fashion · October 2026

Price positioning in fashion

Question
Where does each major fashion chain in Spain sit on price, and who does it overlap with?
Result
Each step up Inditex's brand ladder costs roughly twice as much; Lefties and Primark overlap 97% in jeans.
